## Photos shared in chat (not stored here)

**Sunny valley trail.** Tall second-growth Douglas fir: trunks bare for the lower third or more, narrow, ragged crowns
made of separate foliage clumps rather than smooth tiers. Bright yellow-green grass, sword ferns and mossy boulders
along a dirt trail. Blue sky with cumulus, hard sunlight and dappled shadow, a forested ridge behind.
→ Valley trees need higher, narrower, clumpier crowns with visible trunks. We need a sunny weather state with shadows.

**Subalpine firs by a trail.** Very narrow spires that are foliage all the way to the ground, dark blue-green and densely
textured. Irregular edges and double tops, standing in a meadow on a sandy trail, under a hazy sky.
→ Near the snowline, trees become slim spires with no visible trunk. Silhouettes should be irregular, not perfect cones.

**Snowy corridor (ski trail).** Tall hemlock and fir with long drooping branches. Snow sits in clumps on top of the
branches, and the undersides stay dark. Bright misty overcast at the end of the corridor.
→ Snow on trees should be clumps on branch tops, not even white bands. Droop matters, and so does mist in depth.
